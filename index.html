Program operations experience

Hands-on program work informs the systems I design and the practical questions I ask before proposing a technical solution.

I coordinated mail-order syringe service program operations, including receiving requests, organizing supplies, preparing packages, shipping, and maintaining records. That work made the workflow and its dependencies visible: accurate requests, available inventory, consistent fulfillment, and organized information all have to connect.

Harmonize Hub grew from thinking through those operational needs. I draw on that experience to design around the actual work rather than assuming a tool is useful simply because it has features.

SOAR website administration

A preliminary review of SOAR’s public website, followed by a proposed maintenance approach. This is an independent review, not work performed for SOAR.

PUBLIC SITE · THESOARINITIATIVE.ORG

Keep the information people depend on accessible and current.

SOAR’s website is more than an organizational homepage. It connects visitors to overdose alerts, anonymous reporting, test-strip information, naloxone, videos, and community resources.

01 / PUBLIC INFORMATION

Content & updates

Update announcements, resource information, pages, images, and links as requested, with attention to accuracy and clarity.

02 / USER PATHS

Forms & links

After relevant changes, verify that key paths still work, including reporting, ordering, alert information, and resource navigation.

03 / MAINTENANCE

Access & upkeep

Review ownership, administrator access, update practices, backups, hosting, and vendor dependencies before assuming responsibility or changing anything.

04 / COMMUNICATION

Request-to-completion

Provide a direct contact, acknowledge requests, clarify scope when needed, document changes, and confirm completion.

How I would start

  • 1
    Review the setup
    Identify website platform, hosting, domain and account ownership, access, backups, and vendor dependencies.
  • 2
    Establish a baseline
    Check the site’s public pages, important links, forms, mobile layout, and existing maintenance state.
  • 3
    Agree on scope
    Clarify what routine updates and maintenance include, what needs approval, and what is referred to another provider.
  • 4
    Maintain and verify
    Handle agreed work, verify the result, and keep a simple change record.

A practical arrangement

Routine website updates and upkeep with a local point of contact. The scope and monthly rate would be agreed after understanding the current setup and what SOAR actually needs. Hosting, advanced security, integrations, or broader IT support would not be assumed.

This review is limited to the public-facing website. I have not inspected SOAR’s WordPress dashboard, hosting, security configuration, backups, or third-party systems.
