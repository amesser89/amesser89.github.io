A little about how I work

I tend to notice the friction: the step everyone works around, the information that gets copied three times, the process that only makes sense because one person remembers how it works.

I'm interested in making that stuff clearer and more dependable. Sometimes the fix is technical. Sometimes it's documentation, a better workflow, or simply taking the time to understand the actual problem before changing anything.

Useful technology should make the work easier to do, easier to understand, and harder to lose track of.
